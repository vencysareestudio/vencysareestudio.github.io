What we do

Our services

Every saree is steamed, pleated to your height and drape style, and pinned so it holds until you wear it.

Pre-pleating

Front pleats and pallu pleats set and pressed to your measurements. Just tuck and pin.

Box folding

The pleated saree is folded flat into a neat box shape that holds the pleats in storage.

Saree rolling

Pleated and rolled to pack in a suitcase without creasing. Popular for destination weddings.

Kerala kasavu & set sarees

Crisp, even pleats for kasavu and cotton sarees for Onam, Vishu, temple visits and weddings.

Bridal & heavy silk

Extra care for Kanchipuram, Banarasi and heavy zari work, so the border lines up on the pallu.

Bulk & family orders
